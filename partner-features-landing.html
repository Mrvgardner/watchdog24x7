The Challenge

Excessive fallback transactions expose ATM operators to compliance risk and potential penalties from card networks. Damaged chip cards, faulty readers, and terminal issues force magstripe processing and trigger network thresholds.

The Solution

Monitor and manage fallback transaction rates to stay below network thresholds, avoiding elevated risk flags and potential penalties.

A fallback transaction happens when a chip-enabled card is used, but the transaction is processed through magstripe instead. Common causes include damaged chip cards, faulty card readers, or terminal issues.
  • Visa threshold: 1% at the terminal level — one problem terminal can trigger elevated risk.
  • Mastercard threshold: 2% across the ISO's total platform — broader trends matter across the network.
